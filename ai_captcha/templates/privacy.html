Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

1. Data Collection

When you interact with AI CAPTCHA, we collect minimal technical telemetry necessary to evaluate challenge verification attempts. This includes IP address, User-Agent strings, submission timestamps, and challenge interaction timing metrics.

2. How We Use Data

Telemetry data is used exclusively to evaluate whether verification attempts originate from authorized clients or automated bots, protect against abuse, and maintain service security.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade telemetry data to third parties. Data is processed solely to operate and secure the AI CAPTCHA service.

4. Contact

For inquiries regarding data privacy or security, please consult the service documentation or repository maintainers.
